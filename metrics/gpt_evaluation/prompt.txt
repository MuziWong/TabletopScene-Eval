Judge appearance quality separately from whether the candidate matches the reference's object count and layout, which belong primarily to IA. Do not reward a different background or lighting style by itself.

IA — Image Alignment
Compare the candidate with the reference in object categories and count, relative size, pose, placement, spatial arrangement, and visible appearance/style.
